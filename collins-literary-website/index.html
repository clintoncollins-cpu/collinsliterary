Good questions

Clear answers before we begin.

Can I book one service instead of a full launch?

Yes. Start with the piece that is holding your book back, then add support as your plan grows.

Do you work with indie authors?

Yes. We work with debut authors, series writers and small presses across fiction and non-fiction.

How early should I get in touch?

Earlier is useful for a launch, but we can also help tighten an existing book page or campaign.

Can you work with my existing cover?

Absolutely. We can build the store page, content and promotion around assets you already love.

How we work with you

The same five steps whether you are launching a debut or growing a backlist.

  1. Discover

    We read your book, learn your readers and agree what success looks like.

  2. Position

    We settle your genre, categories, keywords and the message everything else will repeat.

  3. Produce

    Covers, video, store pages and graphics are made and approved with you.

  4. Launch

    Ads, outreach, ARC readers and social posts go live on a planned schedule.

  5. Sustain

    We check the numbers, adjust, and keep your book visible after launch week.
