Privacy policy

Effective September 19, 2026

Collins Literary (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it and the choices you have. If you have questions, write to Collinssliterary@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you use the contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address, book details and the message you write.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and provide the services you ask for.
  • To send you project updates, quotes and invoices if you become a client.
  • To keep the website secure and understand how it is used.

We do not sell your personal information. We only send you marketing emails if you have asked to receive them, and every such email will include a way to unsubscribe.

Contact form

The contact form on this site is submitted through Formspree, which forwards your enquiry to us. Formspree processes the information you provide according to its own privacy policy.

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not set advertising cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers and your IP address may be shared with them. If we add analytics, social media embeds or a newsletter sign-up in future, we will update this policy to name those services.

Sharing your information

We share information only with people and services that help us run our business, such as our email provider, project tools and freelance team members who work on your project, and only as needed. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails and client records for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal and accounting obligations, then delete them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to how we use it. Email us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

External links

Our pages may link to other websites, such as Amazon, Goodreads and social media platforms. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
